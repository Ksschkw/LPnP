Transforming Nigeria's $1.4 trillion informal service economy through trust and technology

The Vision

Create Africa's most trusted peer-to-peer marketplace where social validation replaces anonymous transactions, enabling anyone to safely buy and sell services within their community.

Solution Overview

Three layers of trust that transform how Nigerians connect and transact

Social Vouching

Services need community endorsements before going live. Friends, past clients, colleagues vouch for your skills, building credibility before your first transaction.

Identity Verification

NIN verification via QoreID for all sellers. Real-time government database checks with auto-filled verified profile information.

Secure Transactions

Payments held in escrow until work is completed. Both parties must confirm satisfaction before fund release, with dispute resolution for issues.

Real-Time Matching Engine

Finds nearest available providers in under 2 minutes using advanced geospatial algorithms that consider both proximity (70%) and trust scores (30%).
