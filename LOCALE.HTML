Vision & Problem Statement

Empowering trust, transparency, and local credibility in Africa’s digital economy.

The Vision

LocaLe envisions a Nigeria (and Africa) where trust isn’t dependent on central institutions, but validated through community. The goal is to digitize and scale the same social trust networks that power the informal economy — turning “someone I know” into verified, scalable reputation data.

The Problem

  • Trust gap: People don’t trust strangers online, yet informal services rely on trust.
  • Identity fragmentation: Millions lack verifiable digital identity.
  • Informal economy exclusion: Most trades and services happen outside digital visibility or protection.

Solution Overview

LocaLe builds a “trust layer” for peer-to-peer transactions through three reinforcing pillars.

1. Verified Identity

Every verified user links to a unique national ID (NIN) via QoreID. Real humans, real accountability. Verified users gain higher visibility and trust multiplier effects.

2. Social Vouching

Trust doesn’t start from zero. Friends, past clients, and community members can vouch for your credibility, creating an initial “trust graph.” The more diverse and credible the vouches, the faster you grow.

3. Escrow Protection

Secure payment system holds funds until both parties confirm satisfaction. Automated dispute handling ensures fairness without heavy bureaucracy.

Core Features & Architecture

Frontend

Vue.js SPA with offline-first support, service worker caching, and responsive components optimized for low-end devices.

Backend

FastAPI microservices with JWT auth, async database operations, and modular architecture supporting future scaling to microservices.

Database

PostgreSQL (core) + PostGIS for geospatial data, Redis for caching live sessions, and Celery for background task processing.

Integrations

  • QoreID — Identity Verification
  • Paystack/Flutterwave — Escrow & Payments
  • Termii/Twilio — Messaging
